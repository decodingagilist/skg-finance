About SKG Finance

We Work for You, Not the Banks

SKG Finance was founded on a simple premise: every Australian deserves access to expert, unbiased financial guidance. We are an independent lending brokerage that represents your interests — not the banks'.

Our experienced brokers take the time to understand your unique financial situation and goals before presenting options. We do the legwork across 50+ lenders so you don't have to.

  • Fully independent — we are not owned by any bank or lender
  • Transparent fee disclosure — you always know what you're paying
  • Ongoing support beyond settlement — we're your long-term finance partner
  • Licensed and regulated — full compliance with ASIC requirements

Our Mission

To make expert financial advice accessible, transparent and stress-free for every Australian — whether you're buying your first home or your tenth investment property.

How We Get Paid

We are typically paid a commission by the lender when your loan settles. We will always disclose our commission upfront. Our goal is to find you the best deal — our reputation depends on it.

Our Lender Panel

We partner with all major banks, mutual banks, non-bank lenders and specialist credit providers — giving you genuine choice and competitive rates.
